## Design references (not shipped)
`reference image/*.png` (34 mockups provided by the owner) were used as **visual guidance only**. They are AI-style mockups with people, jewellery and
interiors of unknown provenance, so **no pixel from them is used on the website**. Panels inside them (sitemap, colour palette, typography) are annotations and are not site sections.
Items in the mockups deliberately **not** reproduced because they would be fabricated or unspecified: named customer testimonial and star rating, a social-media photo feed,
"Continue with Google / Apple", a newsletter signup, invented artisan/experience counts, an invented store address and opening hours, "Free shipping across India"
(the real rule is a configurable free-delivery threshold).

## What the owner must supply (see OWNER_SETUP.md)
- Product photography (front, back, detail, model) with alt text, for every product; category banner images; hero imagery.
- Official logo (SVG/PNG) and favicon.
- Brand story copy and any craftsmanship photography **you own or have licensed**.
- Record the source/licence of every supplied image in the table below when it is added.
